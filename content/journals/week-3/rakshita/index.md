## 👩‍💻 Rakshita Pawar

### Week 3: Planning Smarter Social Media

*Social Media Manager · Internship Journal, Week 3 · Research, Content Preparation & Instagram Strategy*

Week 3 focused on organising the information and resources needed to manage PTHOTH Labs' social media more effectively. My work included collecting the social media profiles of the interns who were present, exploring open-source resources, researching suitable websites for students, preparing videos on time, seeking approval before posting, and studying Instagram's algorithm to make more informed decisions about posting dates, times, and video sequence.

#### Collecting Social Media Profiles from Interns

I collected the social media profile links of the interns who were present. This helped bring the team's profiles together in one place and gave me a clearer view of the platforms each intern uses. Having these details organised can make future collaboration, tagging, sharing, and cross-promotion easier when it is relevant to PTHOTH Labs' work.

#### Exploring Open-Source Resources

I explored open-source tools and resources that could be useful for social media work. This included looking at options that may support content creation, video editing, scheduling, design, and basic analytics. The aim was to identify practical resources that could help the team work efficiently and support a more organised content workflow.

#### Researching Suitable Websites for Students

I researched websites and digital platforms that could be useful for students, especially resources that support learning through interactive activities, educational content, and simple digital tools. While exploring these options, I considered whether the platforms were easy to navigate, relevant to students' learning needs, and suitable for the kind of engaging, visual learning experience PTHOTH Labs aims to encourage.

#### Preparing Videos and Seeking Permission

I worked on getting the required videos ready on time so they could be reviewed before publication. Before posting, I shared the videos with sir and asked for permission, following the team's approval process. This helped ensure that the content was checked and aligned with the intended message before it went live.

#### Studying the Instagram Algorithm

I spent time studying how Instagram's recommendation and ranking systems influence the content people see. The goal was to understand the main factors that can affect visibility and engagement, then use that learning to plan the timing, day, and sequence of PTHOTH Labs' videos more thoughtfully. #### Key Takeaways for Content Planning

- **Content relevance:** Create content that matches the interests and needs of the intended audience.
- **Engagement:** Useful content can encourage meaningful likes, comments, shares, saves, and viewing time.
- **Timeliness:** Consider when the audience is likely to be active, then review performance rather than relying on a fixed universal posting time.
- **Relationships and interests:** People may be more likely to see content from accounts they interact with and topics they engage with.
- **Quality and consistency:** Use clear visuals, a strong opening, and a consistent sequence of posts while keeping content original and appropriate.

#### Learning & Outcome

This week helped me connect the creative side of social media management with planning and research. Collecting intern profiles supported team coordination, while exploring open-source tools and student-focused websites helped me look for useful resources. Preparing videos and asking for approval reinforced the importance of a reliable publishing process. Studying Instagram's algorithm also showed me why content planning should consider audience interest, timing, format, and engagement instead of posting without a strategy.

#### Next Steps

- Keep intern profile information organised for relevant collaboration and tagging.
- Shortlist useful open-source tools and student-friendly educational websites for further review.
- Prepare videos in advance and continue getting approval before publishing.
- Plan posting days, times, and video sequence, then observe results and adjust the plan.
- Keep PTHOTH Labs' content useful, clear, consistent, and aligned with its educational purpose.

#### Week 3 Reflection

Effective social media work is not only about publishing a post. It also involves research, preparation, coordination, approval, and learning how an audience discovers and responds to content. Week 3 was a step toward building that more thoughtful and organised process for PTHOTH Labs.
